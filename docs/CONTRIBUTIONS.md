## Cited theorems

We cite Atkinson's formula, Iwaniec's sparse fourth moment, Bourgain's exponent
pair, and Heath-Brown's derivative theorem. The supporting paper proves the
reduction steps. It states the Vinogradov mean-value theorem used in the
derivative estimate.

The eighth-moment paper uses the k=4 line of de Faveri-Pandey. The supporting
paper derives its upper-amplitude bound from the model. The supplement derives
a k=3 line for the alternative eighth-moment proof.
