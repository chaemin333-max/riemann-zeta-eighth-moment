# Release notes

## Version 1.0.0, 17 November 2026

First public release.

- Eighth-moment paper, `paper/moments.pdf`: exponent 76024973/51678924.
  The integer-chord and thin-energy proofs are in Section 2 and the
  stationary-support proof is in Section 3.
- Supporting paper, `sixth-moment/moments.pdf`: sixth-moment exponent
  84599/68640, with full derivations of the parts used from Paper 38 and of
  the reductions of de Faveri and Pandey.
- Supplement, `supplement/moments.pdf`: eighth-moment exponent 357082/242699
  and sixth-moment exponent 143/116 from the sparse fourth moment, mixed-energy
  bounds, and two steps toward the sparse fourth moment.
- Two-page summary, `summary/summary.pdf`.
- Exact checks in `checks/`. The rational outputs use UTF-8 and LF. Optional
  numerical reruns write to `verification-output/`.
- `SHA256SUMS` lists the hashes of the release files.

Author: Min Chae. The record comparison lists the sources, versions and date.
