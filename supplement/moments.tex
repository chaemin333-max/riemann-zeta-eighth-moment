\begin{abstract}
We give an eighth-moment bound with exponent $357082/242699$ using
direct derivative estimates and the sparse fourth-moment theorem.
We also give mixed-energy estimates and two reductions for the sparse
fourth moment. The common curved-phase proofs are in the supporting
paper, and the thin-energy proofs are in the eighth-moment paper.
\end{abstract}
